### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **Informatics Engineering student** at **Politeknik Negeri Malang (Polinema)** with a strong focus on bridging **software engineering** with **applied artificial intelligence and workflow automation**.

- 🔭 **Currently Building**: [Nalarin.ai](https://github.com/M-Aqilaz/Nalarin.ai) (LLM-powered learning platform) & [MAPECO](https://m-aqilaz.github.io) (Geospatial intelligence & automated n8n pipeline).
- 🚀 **Full-Stack Architecture**: Shipped scalable systems using **Laravel 13** & **Next.js 16**, asynchronous queue workers, WebSockets (**Laravel Reverb**), and relational schema modeling with **Prisma ORM & MySQL**.
- 🤖 **AI & ML Integration**: Connecting production apps with token-based LLM APIs (OpenRouter/OpenAI) and training NLP text classification models via **Scikit-Learn & Python**.
- ⚡ **Workflow Automation**: Orchestrating resilient ETL and data pipelines with **n8n**, automated RSS feed ingestion, and webhooks.
- 🎮 **Community & Leadership**: Former **Vice Head of Game Development** & 3D Art Mentor at *Workshop Riset Informatika (WRI) Polinema*.
